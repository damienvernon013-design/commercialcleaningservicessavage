Red Flags to Watch Out for When Choosing a Commercial Cleaner

The gaps in a mediocre provider show up later — in inconsistent service, mystery charges, or constant staff turnover.

Most commercial cleaning companies present well in a sales conversation. The gaps show up later — in inconsistent service, mystery charges, or staff turnover that means a different crew every other week.

Red Flags Before You Sign

  • No written scope of work: If a company quotes you verbally but won't put the task list in writing, there's no accountability.
  • Unusually low pricing: Quotes significantly below market rate usually mean something is missing.
  • No proof of insurance: Any legitimate cleaning company can produce a current certificate of insurance quickly. Hesitation is a sign they don't have it.
  • Vague answers about who will clean your space: If they can't tell you whether staff are employees or subcontractors, proceed with caution.
  • High-pressure sales tactics: “This price is only good until Friday” is a manipulation tactic, not an indicator of a reputable business.
  • No references available: Any company with legitimate commercial accounts should be able to provide references from similar businesses.

Red Flags During the Relationship

  • Constant staff turnover: A different crew every few weeks means no institutional knowledge of your space.
  • Ignored complaints: A company that takes more than a day to respond to a service complaint will not improve over time.
  • Skipped tasks you've paid for: If items on your scope of work are regularly missed, document them and raise the issue formally.
  • Surprise charges on invoices: Additions that weren't agreed to in writing are a sign of billing practices that will escalate.
  • Reluctance to walk through the space with you: A transparent provider should welcome periodic walkthroughs to review quality together.
