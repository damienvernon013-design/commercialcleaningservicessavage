How Often Should a Commercial Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

Getting frequency right means you're not overpaying for services you don't need or undercleaning until things deteriorate.

This is one of the most common questions from business owners shopping for commercial cleaning. The answer depends on several factors — and getting it right means you're not overpaying for services you don't need or undercleaning until things deteriorate.

The General Rule of Thumb

For most office environments, two to three cleaning visits per week strikes the right balance between hygiene, cost, and necessity. High-traffic offices with more than 20 employees or client-facing spaces typically need daily service.

Factors That Determine Frequency

Number of employees: More people means more mess, more high-touch surfaces, more restroom usage.

Client traffic: If clients or customers visit your space regularly, cleanliness is tied to your brand. Daily service is usually worth it.

Industry: Medical and dental facilities require daily or even multiple-daily cleaning by industry standards.

Layout: Open-plan offices with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices with fewer shared touchpoints.

Seasons: Minnesota winters bring salt, slush, and cold-and-flu season — both are good reasons to temporarily increase frequency.

Frequency by Business Type

  • Small professional office (under 10 people): 1–2x per week
  • Mid-size office (10–30 people): 2–3x per week
  • Large open-plan office (30+): daily
  • Medical or dental office: daily, plus deep sanitization protocols
  • Light industrial or distribution facility: varies by shift pattern and floor type
