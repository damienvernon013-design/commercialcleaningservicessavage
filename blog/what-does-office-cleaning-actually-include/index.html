What Does a Commercial Office Cleaning Service Actually Include?

What's included in your quote varies widely between providers — know exactly what you're getting.

Not all commercial cleaning services are the same, and what's included in your quote varies widely between providers. Before you sign a contract, you should know exactly what tasks are — and aren't — part of your standard service.

Standard Inclusions in Most Commercial Cleaning Contracts

  • Vacuuming: All carpeted areas, including under desks and in corners
  • Hard floor mopping: Tile, VCT, sealed concrete — swept and mopped
  • Trash removal: All bins emptied and relined with new bags
  • Restroom cleaning: Toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked
  • Kitchen/breakroom: Counters, sink, microwave exterior, stovetop if applicable
  • Surface dusting: Desks, shelves, window ledges, and common surfaces
  • Glass and mirrors: Interior glass and glass partitions
  • Lobby and reception: Chairs, tables, and floor cleaned

Common Add-Ons (Usually Priced Separately)

  • Floor stripping, waxing, and buffing
  • Exterior window washing
  • High dusting (vents, top of shelving)
  • Post-construction cleanup
  • Event cleanup before or after
  • Supply restocking as a consumables package

What to Ask Your Provider

When reviewing a quote, ask the provider to walk you through every task they will and won't perform. Request a written scope-of-work document rather than a verbal agreement. This protects you if quality ever falls short and gives you a clear basis for evaluating their performance. See our scope of work template.

Customization Is Standard

Good commercial cleaning companies will tailor their services to your space. If you have a server room that should never be entered, a conference room used only once a week, or a kitchen with specific equipment, tell your provider.
