Cleaning Considerations for Shared Community and Multi-Tenant Spaces

Multi-tenant and shared community facilities carry a higher standard of care for shared surfaces.

Buildings that host multiple tenants, or community-facing spaces where different groups rotate through the same rooms, carry a cleaning standard that's different from a single-tenant private office. Shared lobbies, shared restrooms, and shared meeting or common spaces all see a wider range of occupants passing through.

Why Shared Community Spaces Are Higher Risk

Because occupants change throughout the day or week, there's less consistency in who's touching what, and less ability for any single tenant to control hygiene practices in shared areas. That makes the cleaning program the primary line of defense against the spread of illness between unrelated groups sharing the same building.

Key Cleaning Areas in Multi-Tenant and Community Facilities

  • Shared lobbies and entrances: Daily disinfection of door handles, push bars, and reception surfaces
  • Shared restrooms: Sanitized multiple times daily given higher and more varied usage
  • Common meeting or gathering spaces: Table and seating sanitization between bookings or use periods
  • Elevators and stairwells: Buttons, railings, and high-touch points disinfected multiple times daily
  • Shared kitchens or break areas: Counter and appliance disinfection on a schedule that matches actual traffic, not a fixed assumption
