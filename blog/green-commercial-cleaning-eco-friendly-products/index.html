Green Commercial Cleaning: Why Eco-Friendly Products Are Better for Your Business

Green commercial cleaning isn't just the right thing to do — increasingly, it's the smarter business choice.

The commercial cleaning industry uses an enormous volume of chemical cleaning products. Many contain compounds that contribute to indoor air pollution and can trigger allergies and asthma. Green commercial cleaning is the growing alternative — and it's increasingly the smarter business choice, not just the more responsible one.

What Makes a Cleaning Product “Green”?

  • Ingredients evaluated for human health and environmental safety
  • Industry-recognized certifications for institutional and industrial cleaners
  • Relevance to LEED-pursuing buildings
  • Biodegradable formulations that break down naturally

Health Benefits for Your Employees

Many conventional cleaning products release compounds that contribute to headaches, dizziness, and fatigue tied to indoor air quality. Green products reduce that exposure. For businesses with staff who have asthma or chemical sensitivities, switching to green cleaning is often a meaningful quality-of-life improvement.

Business Benefits

  • Brand alignment: If your business has sustainability commitments, your cleaning program should reflect them
  • Building certifications: Green cleaning programs can contribute to sustainability certification scores
  • Client perception: Corporate clients and employees look favorably on businesses that demonstrate environmental responsibility
  • Reduced liability: Fewer harsh chemicals means reduced risk of chemical-related incidents
