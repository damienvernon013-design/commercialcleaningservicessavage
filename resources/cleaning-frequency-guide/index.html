Nightly, 3x Week or Weekly: Choosing a Frequency

How to match your cleaning schedule to your actual building use, not a contractor’s preference for a higher contract value.

Cleaning frequency is the decision that most affects the monthly cost of a commercial cleaning contract, and it is one that contractors have a financial interest in pushing upward. This guide gives you a framework for choosing a frequency that matches your actual building use rather than defaulting to whatever the contractor recommends.

Nightly Service (5 Nights Per Week)

Nightly cleaning is appropriate for facilities that have daily employee presence, regular client or visitor traffic, and a high proportion of shared spaces. The benchmark is a facility where you would notice a cleaning gap on a Tuesday morning if Monday night’s service was missed. Examples: a 10-person professional office with shared kitchen and restrooms, a medical suite with patient traffic six days per week, or a retail office combination with daily public access.

In the Savage market, nightly service is the standard for office buildings in the McColl Drive corridor and along County Road 42 where tenants typically arrive by 8:00 AM and need the building ready. It is also standard for the light industrial suites on the west side of Savage that run one shift with a 6:00 PM cutoff, giving a clear cleaning window each evening.

Three Nights Per Week (Typically M/W/F)

Three-night service is appropriate for offices with lower daily traffic, smaller teams, or occupants who are frequently off-site. The test: if you were in the office only three days per week and had a team of five or fewer, is nightly cleaning adding value? The honest answer for most small offices is no. The facility does not generate enough soil between Monday and Tuesday to require a Tuesday night service.

Three-night service is common in the Prior Lake and Chanhassen markets for professional services firms (accounting, legal, insurance) where the team is in the office inconsistently and the building use pattern simply does not warrant daily cleaning. It is also appropriate for second-story office suites without a street-level lobby, where the floor condition changes less overnight than a high-traffic ground-floor space.

Weekly or Twice-Weekly Service

Weekly cleaning is appropriate for very small offices (under 1,500 sq ft), storage facilities with occasional occupancy, or facilities that are cleaning as a lease compliance requirement rather than as an active need. Twice-weekly is a common pattern for small warehouse offices where the office portion requires cleaning but the production floor is swept by the client’s own staff.

If a contractor recommends nightly service for a 900 sq ft office with two employees, the recommendation is not in your interest. That is a once-per-week or twice-per-week account by any reasonable standard.

Special Considerations for the South Metro

Minnesota winters change the frequency calculus for entry areas. From November through March, salt and grit from the parking lot enters at every entry point. Facilities that would otherwise be fine on a three-night schedule sometimes add a Tuesday and Thursday entry touch-up from November through April to manage the winter floor condition without triggering a full nightly program upgrade. This is a common and cost-effective option for mid-size offices in the Savage and Burnsville corridor.
