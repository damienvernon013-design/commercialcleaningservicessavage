What Happens Next

When you submit this form, the request is routed to a crew coordinator who covers the South Metro. You will receive a callback by the next business day to confirm the details and schedule a walkthrough if needed.

For most facilities under 10,000 sq ft, we can produce a written quote based on the information in this form without a site visit. Larger or more complex facilities typically require a short walkthrough to assess floor surface conditions and restroom count before pricing can be finalized.

What the Quote Includes

Every written quote from Savage Commercial Cleaning Services includes the service description, the cleaning frequency, the scheduled days and access times, and the monthly cost. It also includes a certificate of insurance on request. The quote is not a binding contract until both parties sign the scope of work, and there is no pressure to sign on the first call.
