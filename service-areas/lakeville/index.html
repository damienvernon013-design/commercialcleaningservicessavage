The Lakeville Commercial Market

Lakeville (population approximately 72,000) has a commercial base defined by the Dodd Boulevard commercial corridor, the Airlake Industrial Park at the southern edge of the city—home to distribution, light manufacturing, and logistics tenants—and the I-35 interchange development that has brought hotel, retail, and office uses to the north end of Lakeville. These are not generic descriptions—they determine the cleaning requirements specific to this market. Distribution tenants on forklift-active floors need different floor care protocols than medical suites. Office buildings with 8:00 AM tenant arrival need a cleaning window that ends by 7:45 AM. We have seen the full range of building types in Lakeville and know what each one requires.
