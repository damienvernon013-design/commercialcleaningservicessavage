Why Savage Buildings Choose Us

Not the cheapest. Not the flashiest. The contractor who shows up and can be reached when something goes wrong.

The typical facility manager has tried at least two cleaning contractors before calling us. The reasons for switching are almost always the same: inconsistent service, no accountability structure, and no written scope that either party could point to when things went wrong.

We are not asking you to trust us based on that observation. We are asking you to look at the structure and decide whether it is more accountable than what you have now. If you are currently under contract elsewhere, see our guide on switching commercial cleaning companies without downtime.

22 Years in Business

Cleaning companies in this market average less than five years before they fold or are acquired. A contractor with 22 years of operation in the South Metro has weathered multiple business cycles, maintained a crew through labor shortages, and retained enough accounts to still be operating. That is not a marketing claim. It is a fact you can verify by asking when the company started and doing the math.

Written Scope of Work on Every Account

Every account we take on gets a written scope before the first service date. The scope defines what is cleaned, how often, by which method, and what happens when a service falls short. A contractor who will not put the scope in writing is a contractor who cannot be held to a standard. We put it in writing because we intend to meet it.

Assigned Crew, Not a Dispatch Pool

Many cleaning contractors in the South Metro operate a dispatch model—whoever is available that night goes to your building. We assign a specific crew to each account. The crew lead knows your building, your preferences, and your alarm protocol. When the crew changes, you are notified before the change happens.

General Liability Insurance and Bonding

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations, and every crew member is bonded. A certificate of insurance is available with every written quote. You do not have to ask for it twice. See the insured and bonded page for the full detail.

Honest Pricing

We do not quote low and renegotiate at 90 days. The written quote reflects what the service actually costs for your facility. If the scope changes, the price changes—but only with your written agreement. See the pricing page for the framework.
