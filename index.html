Who We Serve

Built for the Buyer Who Manages the Building

Most of the inquiries we receive come from operations managers, office managers, and property managers—people who answer for the condition of the building without necessarily doing the cleaning themselves. They have tried at least one other contractor. They know what a missed service costs in complaints and credibility.

What they need is not enthusiasm. They need a written scope, a real price, a crew that shows up, and a number to call when something is wrong. That is exactly the structure this service runs on.

The Savage Market

Savage sits on the Scott County side of the Minnesota River corridor, bordered by Prior Lake, Shakopee, and Burnsville. The city’s commercial base is concentrated along Highway 13 and the McColl Drive corridor, with additional office and light industrial stock near the intersection of County Road 42. The Murdock Road industrial area houses distribution and manufacturing tenants whose facilities require cleaning between shifts or after business hours, not during them. We are familiar with the access requirements, dock schedules, and floor conditions common to that class of building.

We serve all of Savage and the communities within 25 miles, including Prior Lake, Burnsville, Shakopee, Bloomington, Eagan, Lakeville, Apple Valley, and Chanhassen.
