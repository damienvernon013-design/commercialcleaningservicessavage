About Savage Commercial Cleaning Services

22 years in the South Metro. Insured, bonded, and built for the buyer who manages the building.

Savage Commercial Cleaning Services has operated in the South Metro for 22 years. The business is focused on one vertical—commercial cleaning for businesses, office buildings, and light industrial facilities—and it does not pretend to be anything else.

What We Do and Do Not Do

We clean commercial facilities. That means offices, multi-tenant buildings, light industrial suites, and mixed-use commercial properties in Savage and the surrounding cities within 25 miles. We do not offer residential cleaning, window washing, HVAC cleaning, or carpet dyeing. Scope clarity matters when you are calling a contractor to handle a building you are responsible for.

How the Business Works

Every engagement starts with a written scope of work. The scope defines the service, the frequency, the access requirements, and the contact chain if something goes wrong. We do not call it a contract to make it sound more important—it is a working document that both sides agree to and that either side can point to when there is a question.

Our crew is assigned to specific buildings, not dispatched from a general pool each night. That means the person cleaning your facility on a Tuesday knows your building, your alarm code procedure, and your preferences. When a crew member changes, you are notified in advance.

22 Years in the South Metro

The South Metro commercial market has changed substantially over 22 years. Savage, Prior Lake, and Shakopee have added substantial commercial and light industrial stock along the Highway 13 and 169 corridors. We have cleaned facilities in all of these markets through growth cycles and slowdowns, and the thing that has not changed is the expectation of the buyer: show up, do the work, and be reachable when something is not right.

The quote is free, the scope is written, and the callback is next-business-day guaranteed. That is the structure.
