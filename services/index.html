Commercial Cleaning Services in Savage, MN

Three core services, written scopes, and 22 years serving the South Metro.

Every service we provide starts with a written scope of work specific to your facility. The scope defines what is cleaned, how often, with what method, and by whom. It is the document that makes a cleaning contract enforceable and a service complaint resolvable.

Nightly Commercial Cleaning

Scheduled nightly or multi-night cleaning covering vacuuming, trash removal, restroom service, and surface sanitation. Built for offices, light industrial, and multi-tenant suites from 1,500 to 50,000 sq ft.

Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance

Stripping, waxing, buffing, and scrubbing for VCT, sealed concrete, and luxury vinyl tile. Scheduled quarterly or on demand. Access and dry-time requirements confirmed before the first service date.

Restroom Sanitation Programs

Documented high-touch disinfection, supply replenishment, and service logging for multi-stall commercial restrooms. Available as a standalone program or combined with nightly cleaning.
