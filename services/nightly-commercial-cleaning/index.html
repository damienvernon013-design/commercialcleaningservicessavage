Nightly Commercial Cleaning in Savage, MN

Scheduled nightly or multi-night service with a written scope, documented crew assignment, and a direct number for service issues.

Nightly commercial cleaning is the foundational service for any facility that opens to employees, clients, or the public on a regular schedule. It is not a single task—it is a defined sequence of tasks tied to a scope of work, completed on a fixed schedule, and verified by a supervisor who can be reached by name.

What the Service Covers

The scope of work for nightly commercial cleaning is written to the specific facility, not pulled from a template. Typical elements include: vacuuming all carpeted areas; damp-mopping hard surface floors; emptying and relining waste receptacles; cleaning and disinfecting restroom fixtures, counters, and touch surfaces; wiping kitchen and breakroom surfaces; cleaning glass entry doors; and spot-cleaning walls and light switches.

For Savage facilities along the McColl Drive corridor or in the Highway 13 commercial zone—offices, light industrial suites, and multi-tenant buildings—nightly service is typically scheduled between 6:00 PM and midnight. Facilities with later operating hours can discuss an adjusted window during the quote process.

Frequency Options

Most commercial tenants in Scott County operate five days per week and need five-night service. Three-night service (typically Monday, Wednesday, Friday) suits smaller offices under 3,000 sq ft or buildings with lower daily traffic. Two-night and weekly schedules are available for storage facilities, seasonal offices, or buildings that are not in continuous use.

Frequency is confirmed in the written scope and does not change without a documented modification. If your building schedule changes, the cleaning schedule adjusts accordingly.

Who It Suits

This service is the right fit for office managers and operations directors who manage between one and ten thousand square feet of commercial space. It is also appropriate for property managers overseeing multi-tenant buildings where each suite requires service on its own schedule. Single-tenant facilities with under 1,500 sq ft sometimes find that a twice-weekly schedule and a supplemental restroom program covers their needs at lower cost than nightly service.

We serve Savage and the South Metro, including Prior Lake, Burnsville, Shakopee, Bloomington, Eagan, Lakeville, Apple Valley, and Chanhassen.

Pricing

Nightly cleaning is priced per visit, with discounts for higher-frequency contracts. The key variables are square footage, number of restrooms, floor surface mix, and desired schedule. See the pricing page for the framework, or request a written quote for your facility.
