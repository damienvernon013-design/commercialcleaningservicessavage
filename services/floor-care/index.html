Floor Care & Hard Surface Maintenance in Savage, MN

Stripping, waxing, buffing, and scrubbing for VCT, sealed concrete, and luxury vinyl tile. Scheduled or on-demand.

Hard surface floors in commercial facilities carry a level of wear that residential products are not designed for. A distribution warehouse on McColl Drive puts forklift traffic on sealed concrete daily. A medical suite sees foot traffic across luxury vinyl tile from the moment the door opens. An office building with VCT tile in its corridors shows every scuff and heel mark under fluorescent light. The floor condition is the first thing a building visitor notices and, in multi-tenant facilities, the first thing a property manager hears about.

What Commercial Floor Care Covers

Our floor care program covers stripping (removing worn finish buildup), applying new finish coats, buffing and burnishing to restore gloss, and periodic scrubbing for deep soil removal. We work on vinyl composite tile (VCT), sealed concrete, luxury vinyl tile (LVT and LVP), and terrazzo. We do not strip and wax hardwood or epoxy-coated floors without a site assessment first, as those surfaces require different chemistry.

Standard VCT programs run on a quarterly or semi-annual strip-and-wax cycle depending on traffic volume, with monthly buffing in between. High-traffic entry corridors may require a supplemental scrub-and-recoat pass mid-cycle. All of this is defined in the scope of work before the first service date.

Scheduling and Access

Floor care requires unobstructed access and appropriate dry time before foot traffic can resume. Most strip-and-wax services are scheduled on Friday nights or weekend mornings to avoid disrupting building operations. We confirm the schedule and any access requirements during the quote process. You will not arrive Monday morning to a wet floor.

For the South Metro
